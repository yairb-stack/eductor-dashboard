What “per motive air” means

η = (total powder flow / total motive-air flow) × clean high-shear outlet fraction

Count the useful powder

A particle must reach the outlet and experience G ≥ 106 s−1. Its contribution is weighted by exp(−Λ), the modeled probability of avoiding a first sticking event. The worst result across 0.3 µm and 10 µm particles sets the score.

Keep the trade-offs visible

First-sticking probability is conditional on outlet arrival. High-shear delivery uses all released particles as the denominator. No minimum high-shear dwell time is required. Entrained air, vacuum power and compressor electricity are not included in this mass-flow objective.

At equal motive air, two lower-pressure bodies can be preferable

Creative geometry: distribute powder around one jet

The design atlas has Blender exterior, cutaway, end and exploded views, dimensions, and downloadable CAD.

Radial concepts predict 0.35–0.42 g/g in selected low-pressure Phase 2 screens, but the alternative transport closure nearly eliminates their useful delivery at the confirmed 3/1 bar setting. They cannot be called the best geometry. Eight 45° feeds near the jet entry were less sensitive (0.193–0.236 g/g at 3/1 bar), so they are a useful development candidate.

A serial cascade is not ranked: it adds air and collision exposure, and requires interstage particle transfer and recombination to be modeled. The next useful design step is a short, controlled shear encounter with prompt exit.

Acceptance requires finite positive native fields, mass imbalance below 1%, port-flow drift below 5%, velocity-field drift below 5% and shear-field drift below 10%. Earlier transient attempts either failed numerically or were stopped before their requested physical horizon; they are retained as failed/incomplete, never as validation.

All three final runs failed the conservation / convergence gates, so particle tracking on these fields was not treated as validation. The next CFD correction is a pressure-coupled compressible balance with audited port signs, followed by mesh and stationarity checks. Even a passing gas screen would not establish particle coagulation: the collision kernel, sticking efficiency and concentration history need separate validation. First-order reconstruction on one mesh also cannot establish a grid-independent shear maximum.

What could change the recommendation?

Transport and concentration

Confirm how particles enter the jet shear layer, especially at radial junctions. Focusing the release lanes currently changes positions without increasing local concentration as a real concentrated powder sheet might. Resolve the feed manifold and particle concentration before manufacturing a new body.

Coagulation physics

The study uses a Fuchs Brownian + WZW turbulent first-event model, sticking efficiency 1 and a frozen particle background. Much of the turbulent dose is outside the kernel's applicability screen. The probabilities are comparative model estimates, not validated final agglomerate fractions.

Inputs, weighting and reproducibility

Separate monodisperse 0.3 and 10 µm particles; density 2200 kg/m³; 20°C. Three equal-weight release lanes per size, with flux weighting across branches. Confirmation runs use 48 ms horizons and two independent seeds. Native nozzle discharge coefficients are 0.82 for branched bodies and 0.94 for British bodies. Vessel and outlet pressures are always equal.
