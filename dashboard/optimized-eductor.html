01 / MOTIVE AIR

One jet supplies the energy

Air enters the central insert and contracts to a 3.5 mm exit. The selected analytical setting is 3 bar absolute motive pressure with vessel and outlet both at 1 bar absolute.

01 · DISTRIBUTE

Use the circumference

Eight angled feeds spread the entry positions around one jet. Equal powder splitting would mean 0.208 g/s per feed; the distributor still needs to be designed.

02 · ENCOUNTER

Meet the jet near its exit

Nominal branch axes aim at x = 0. The real nozzle diverts the flow through an annular entry plenum. That added detail can change the analytical prediction.

03 · EXIT

Limit extra collision exposure

The 50 mm mixing length is intended to permit useful shear followed by prompt discharge. Actual residence times and coagulation require a qualified flow solution.

Geometry, in detail

What is inside the housing?

Section through the motive axis

Blue: motive-air guide. Teal: carrier and powder guides. Orange: intended near-jet encounter region. These paths and the highlighted region are schematic, not solved streamlines, shear contours or particle histories.

Nominal dimensions · mm

The nozzle exit defines x = 0. Branches are 45° to the main axis and 45° apart around it. Their nominal 40 mm centerlines intersect at x = 0; they do not represent 40 mm of separate pipe.

Eight distinct inlet ports

Looking downstream from the motive side. Eight 5.66 mm bores provide 201.06 mm² total feed area, equal to a single 16 mm bore. The motive supply bore is also drawn as 16 mm, but belongs to a separate air passage.

Housing and removable nozzle insert

Exploded half-section for visibility. The CAD separates the nozzle and housing without metal overlap. Retention, sealing, connectors and assembly tolerances are not yet specified.

Promising geometry, unfinished flow design

The angled feeds were less sensitive to the tested transport closure than the radial-feed concept. The radial body used for the CFD trial is a different geometry.

The new entry plenum and nozzle shell make the CAD connected and physically separated. They were not included in the analytical screen, and this realization has not been run through OpenFOAM.

None of the three final CFD cases passed conservation and stationarity checks. These images do not turn the analytical shortlist into validated performance.
